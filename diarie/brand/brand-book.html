01 · the idea

One anchor, two motifs

One anchor: the diarium. Every Swedish myndighet keeps one: the registry where each incoming and outgoing handling is recorded, numbered, findable — never hidden. That is offentlighetsprincipen, 1766, the world's first freedom-of-information law, and it maps onto diarie's invariants one to one: a malformed row is represented, never silently dropped; git is the audit log; every task has its diarienummer. The register aesthetic is not decoration on the ethos — it is the ethos, 250 years older than the tool. When the brand must say one thing, it says this.

Supporting motif — herbarium. The -arium sibling: an archive of living things. Linné's specimen sheets are 280-year-old plain files — paper, a pressed flower, a label, a stamp — still readable, no vendor, no format rot. His taxonomy is diarie's type system: one genus per specimen, many descriptors. The dependency graph is the root system; ready is what has broken the soil.

Supporting motif — genomlysning. An archivist examines a sheet by backlighting it on a light table — and genomlysning is also, in Swedish, the word for scrutiny. The halo behind the mark is not an aura in the mystical sense: it is the light table. The glow is the honesty.

Everything below follows from these three layers. Where a visual decision cannot be traced back to one of them, or to an invariant in VISION.md, it does not belong in the system.

02 · the mark

A pressed twinflower, backlit

The mark is Linnaea borealis — named for Linné, his own favorite: a small, modest, Nordic woodland plant. Two nodding bells on one forked stem, which is also the tool's central pair — the CLI reads, your editor writes. Behind it, the light table: a halo in four flat steps.

  • The halo is stepped, never a gradient. This is risograph light, not lens flare — four steps, outer to inner.
  • The flower is papper or arkiv. Cream on dark, ink on paper. No third color, no outline version.
  • Clearspace: one bell-height on all sides. Minimum 24 px for the mark, 140 px for the lockup — below 24 px, use the dedicated favicon cut.
  • Don't detach the bells, tilt the mark, add a drop shadow, or place it on photography.
  • Don't animate it beyond the one sanctioned reveal (chapter 06).
  • Both SVG:s are commented and hand-editable; the lockup's wordmark is Fraunces converted to outlines, so no font is required to open them.

Named honestly, because the reader is honest: the twinflower is not an unclaimed symbol. It appears in Linné's own coat of arms (1757) and in the Linnean Society of London's heraldry — scientific-heritage bodies, not software — and Linnea is among Sweden's most common given names. diarie borrows the specimen; it does not claim the species.

03 · color

The highlights have jobs

Names are Swedish, ASCII-folded in code: arkiv — archive; lysning, from genomlysning — backlighting, scrutiny; papper — paper; hektograf — the violet copying-ink of the analog myndighet; fosfor — terminal phosphor; bärnsten — amber; stämpel — stamp. Contrast ratios are computed, not vibes; re-verify if you change a value.

Each pairing carries two numbers: the WCAG 2.x ratio and the APCA Lc (0.0.98G-4g; negative = light-on-dark). APCA is the stricter, perceptually honest model, and it is stricter here in two places worth naming: hektograf on arkiv scores −22 — decorative and border use only, even at display sizes — and stämpel-ljus scores −46: short status labels, not paragraphs. Where the models disagree, follow APCA.

The rule that keeps the palette from being a mood board: the accents are the exit-code contract, in color. fosfor speaks for the CLI and for nothing else — never use it decoratively where it could be mistaken for “this is workable.”

0The answer is on stdout. An empty but present store is a legitimate answer.
1You asked wrong. ENOSTORE, EUSAGE, EEXIST — correctable, not condemned.
2It ran, and the answer is no. The stamp belongs to validate — the authority that rejects.

One hazard, named plainly because pretending otherwise would be off-brand: dark ground with a single acid-green accent is a default AI aesthetic in 2026. What keeps this palette specific is everything the default lacks — the paper, the stamps, the stepped light, amber and red as full citizens, and accents that are checkable claims against the CLI:s actual exit codes.

05 · voice

Klarspråk

Swedish law requires public language to be vårdat, enkelt och begripligt — cared-for, simple, comprehensible (Språklagen 2009:600, §11) — and a tool named after a myndighet's registry writes like one, at its best. The rules:

  • Name the consequence, not the adjective. The consequence is the part that tells the reader whether to care.
  • The refusal is part of the product. Say no plainly, give the reason, name the revival trigger where one exists.
  • No superlatives. The strongest available sentence is a plain claim: “That is the whole product.”
  • diarie is lowercase. Always. It is a command, not a company.
  • House punctuation: the em-dash pivot and the colon as the seam between claim and evidence — both make the reasoning visible, which is the point of the reasoning.
  • One licensed anthropomorphism: the reader is honest. The tool has no feelings, only obligations.
  • Swedish is seasoning, not gatekeeping — always glossed on first use.

06 · motion

Scarce, and with a job

  • At most three beats per page; the hero owns one. Everything else is functional transition, not spectacle.
  • Never on the install command or any call to action. Motion that leans on a decision is a dark pattern; diarie does not lean.
  • One easing — the light coming up on the table. Never the browser's default ease.
  • Reduced motion is full parity: the complete page, rendered at once. Degrading honestly applies to animation too.
  • Nothing runs longer than two seconds, and below-the-fold beats fire once per session.
07 · applications

Where the system shows up

The CLI itself

Terminal output uses the semantic tokens — and NO_COLOR and non-TTY output are respected without information loss: color is annotation, never the message.

$ diarie stats --stale
14 tasks · 2 ready · 3 blocked · 1 stale claim (proj-auth, 12 days)
$ diarie validate
✗ proj-login: dep "proj-authh" does not exist — did you mean "proj-auth"?

The stamp

Releases carry the diarienummer device: a rectangular date stamp, stamp-red, reading INKOM <date> over dnr <version> — the mark a registrar puts on a handling the day it arrives. It appears on the site footer and in release notes, and nowhere else — a stamp used everywhere certifies nothing. Its sibling granskad (examined) may appear once, on a specimen sheet.

Two smaller devices join it: the marker wash — a hektograf tint over a key phrase, at most three per page, because emphasis is scarce — and the leaf rule, a hairline carrying the mark's opposite leaf-pair, which lets the scene recur between sections.

diarie.dev

One static HTML document. Fonts inlined, zero external requests, no analytics, no cookies, no consent banner because there is nothing to consent to. View source is a feature: the page practices what the tool preaches, and survives curl, file://, and the Internet Archive without modification. The consequence, named: ≈152 KiB of subset fonts become ≈203 KiB of base64 (+33 %), and fonts cannot be cached separately from the page. Orthodoxy says split them; diarie keeps the one file knowingly — the named trigger for revisiting is a measured LCP above 2.5 s on a cold mobile load. One sibling asset exists: og.png, the link-preview card, path-rendered from the mark and wordmark — scrapers fetch it; the page never does.

08 · the refusals, brand edition

Identity, not backlog

no gradient glow no mascot no dark-pattern motion no tracking, ever no third typeface no brand asset behind a login

The halo is stepped or it is not the halo — the sanctioned atmosphere is stepped light at any scale and static print grain (--korn, ≤ 5 % opacity). The twinflower is a specimen, not a character — it does not wave, wink, or wear a hard hat during errors. A registry that surveils its readers has misunderstood which direction the transparency goes. And the brand is files: leaving — forking, re-theming, deleting — must always be free.

09 · lineage

The canon is the author's own

Local-first (Ink & Switch), convivial tools (Illich), calm technology, worse-is-better, indieweb — not borrowed for the occasion but long kept. Maggie Appleton's Home-Cooked Software carries the Illich thread — that people "should have agency and ownership over their data and software." Amber Case's calm-technology work holds the standard the site aims at: the smallest possible claim on attention. And Jeremy Keith's Declarative design names the test diarie applies to itself: "It all depends on whether the philosophy behind the tool matches your own philosophy."
